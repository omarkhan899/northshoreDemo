# NorthShore Pressure Pros

A responsive Next.js homepage demo for a locally owned exterior cleaning business in Northshore Louisiana.

```sh
npm ci
npm run dev
```

Open http://localhost:3000. For a production check, run `npm run build` followed by `npm start`.

Business details, navigation, services, and review placeholders live in `app/content.ts`. The estimate form validates locally and directs visitors to the business phone number.

## Photography and branding

- The driveway comparison and landscaping photos are supplied NorthShore assets. The comparison remains a single image so its original labels and branding are preserved.
- The header and footer use a crop of the actual uniform logo with a readable text wordmark. Both the uniform and business card references are in `public/assets/brand`.
- The hero and house soft-washing service images were made with the built-in image generation tool. These illustrate services. The Work section contains the supplied driveway project.

### Image generation prompts

**Hero.** Photorealistic natural editorial service photography for a full-bleed 2.3:1 Louisiana pressure-washing website hero. An adult male exterior cleaner in a dark navy blue hooded sweatshirt (hood up), tan work pants and black boots is actively pressure washing a residential stone patio with a realistic professional long metal pressure-washing wand held in both hands. His full body is on the right third, facing inward, with clearly visible high-pressure white water fan striking the pavers and believable fine backlit mist, wet clean pavers contrasting with buildup. The left half is dark leafy landscaping and shaded empty patio with low detail for overlaid website typography. Upscale Louisiana suburban home with warm white brick and dark windows to the right, live oak greenery, natural daytime light, warm highlights and realistic shadows, documentary commercial photography, understated colors. Frame the worker's hood, hands, boots and spray with comfortable margins. Landscape panoramic crop, no text, logos, watermark, illustration or 3D.

**House soft washing.** Photorealistic editorial landscape photograph for a Louisiana house soft washing service card. A well-kept light cream Louisiana suburban home with subtle white horizontal siding, blue-gray shutters, brick porch steps, oak shade and clipped green landscaping. The side and front porch are seen at a natural three-quarter angle, with a faint fan of low-pressure water entering from the right toward the siding. Realistic everyday commercial service photography, natural midmorning sunlight, 3:2 crop, no text, no logos, no illustration.
